# Plan: a persistent queue for overdue reminders

## Goal

The reminder job runs on an in-process timer, so a restart loses its place. Move reminders onto a persistent job queue so they survive restarts, and keep the rule that an overdue invoice gets exactly one reminder per day.

## Approach

1. Add a `jobs` array to the data file. A job is `{ id, type: 'reminder', invoiceId, status, runAt }` with status `queued`, `running` or `done`.
2. Once an hour, scan open invoices that are past due and have had no reminder in the last 24 hours, and add a `queued` job for each. Replace `sendReminders` in `src/jobs/reminders.js` with this scan.
3. Start a worker that every second loads the data, picks the oldest `queued` job whose `runAt` has passed, and claims it by setting it to `running` with `store.update`.
4. The worker sends the email with `email.send`, then sets the job to `done` and writes `lastReminderAt` on the invoice, each with its own `store.update`.
5. Remove the old `setInterval` job from `src/server.js`.

## Acceptance criteria

- Restarting the server does not lose queued reminders.
- An overdue invoice gets one reminder email per day.
- Paid or voided invoices get no reminder.

## Verification

Add a test that creates an overdue invoice, runs the scan twice and the worker until it is idle, and asserts one email in the outbox. Run `npm test`.

# Plan: soft-delete customers

## Goal

Deleting a customer currently removes their record and leaves their invoices pointing at nothing. Deleted customers should be hidden from the customer list but keep their history, so their invoices stay meaningful to staff.

## Approach

1. Change `remove(id)` in `src/services/customers.js` so it sets `deletedAt = Date.now()` on the customer instead of filtering the record out of the array.
2. New customers are created with `deletedAt: null`.
3. Change `list()` to return only customers whose `deletedAt === null`, so deleted customers no longer show in `GET /customers`.
4. The existing data file needs no migration: the new field appears on records as they are created or deleted.
5. Invoices of a deleted customer stay in `GET /invoices` for admins, as they are today.
6. Update the README's Ground rules to say customers are soft-deleted.

## Acceptance criteria

- After `DELETE /customers/:id` the customer no longer appears in `GET /customers`.
- The customer's invoices still appear for admins.
- Creating a new customer still works and the new customer appears in the list.

## Verification

Add cases to `test/invoices.test.js`: create a customer, delete it, check that it is missing from `list()` and that its invoice is still returned by `invoices.list()`. Run `npm test`.
